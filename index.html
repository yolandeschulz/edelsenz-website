Yolande Schulz

Managing Director

Yolande founded EdelSenz after more than 18 years in senior corporate executive and regional leadership roles spanning Europe, the Middle East and Africa. That experience taught her what it takes to operate at a global standard while staying locally relevant, delivering fit-for-purpose solutions for the markets EdelSenz serves.

She leads the business with one ambition: to make a real difference in food in Africa through science-backed, ethically sourced and competitively priced ingredient and product solutions.

Ralf Schulz

Director

Ralf is a Director and shareholder of EdelSenz, and Managing Director of Schulz Attorneys, where his practice covers commercial, property and trust law. He brings that governance and commercial expertise to EdelSenz’s strategic oversight, helping steer the company’s growth, investment decisions and long-term partnerships.

Shaney Hansen

Development Chef · Pantry & Home

Shaney is EdelSenz’s Development Chef for the Pantry & Home division and leads artisanal, small-batch manufacturing. She turns seasonal, farm-fresh ingredients into shelf-ready ranges, bridging culinary craft with scalable, repeatable production.

Stephanie de la Rey

Head of Herbs, Spices & Seasonings

Stephanie leads the Herbs, Spices & Seasonings division, driving technical seasoning and flavour development and managing EdelSenz’s sole-distributor partnership with Akay. She brings a background in flavour and product development from Cape Herb & Spice and McCormick & Company, and holds an MSc in food science focused on functional food ingredients.
